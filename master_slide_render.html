📊 SWOT Analysis: AI-Driven Maritime Intelligence
💪 Strengths
  • Early market price prediction protecting against booking losses
  • Physical port compatibility engine (Draft/LOA/Beam) for safe diversion
  • Live ship tracking with real-time port congestion radar
  • Railway rake evacuation alerts for fast port-to-factory transport
⚠️ Weaknesses
  • Reliance on public AIS feeds with ~40 NM coastal latency
  • Limited direct integration with private terminal TOS
  • Periodic reporting lags in historical freight rate indices
🎯 Opportunities
  • Integration with National Logistics Portal (NLP-Marine)
  • Expansion to coastal container and liquid tanker segments
  • Direct integration with live bunker fuel supplier price feeds
🛡️ Threats
  • Satellite AIS telemetry outages or latency fluctuations
  • Entrenched legacy maritime analytics providers
  • Sudden regulatory import duties or customs tariff revisions

⚖️ Feasibility Takeaways:
  • Multi-Source Ingestion: Integrates AIS transponders, port tariffs, and weather bulletins into a single data pipeline.
  • Rapid MVP Build: Complete working route optimizer, berth diversion engine, and live congestion radar.
  • Solar-Lunar Tide Model: Computes high-water tidal windows without relying on gated proprietary APIs.
  • Intermodal Rake Alert: Synchronizes vessel berthing with Indian Railways rake mobilization for rapid coal clearance.
⚙️ Viability Takeaways:
  • Downside Price Protection: Safeguards charterers against locking in fixtures at peak rates before market dips.
  • Demurrage Elimination: Early diversion before multi-day outer anchorage congestion queues escalate.
  • Port Network Scalability: Adaptable across East & West Coast Indian ports (Paradip, Vizag, Haldia, Dhamra, Gangavaram).
  • Sustainable Framework: Extensible data architecture designed for integration with National Logistics Portal (NLP-Marine).
