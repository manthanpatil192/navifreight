SOLUTION
NaviFreight is an end-to-end maritime intelligence and multi-modal supply chain decision-support system engineered for Indian public sector steelmakers (SAIL) to dynamically optimize coal chartering, berth scheduling, rail-truck dispatch, and blast-furnace stockyard resilience.
•
Optimal Market Entry & Tender Timing: Advises SAIL on strategic tender booking windows using P10/P50/P90 market forecasts, dynamically recommending the optimal charter ratio between long-term Contracts of Affreightment (COA) and spot fixtures (e.g., maintaining a baseline ~70% COA to ~30% spot mix, shifting with market volatility).
•
Vessel Bunching & Furnace-Critical Berthing: Prioritizes berth access for bulkers feeding coal-starved steelworks (e.g., Rourkela, Bokaro) while eco-pacing non-critical vessels; evaluates whether to divert to alternate ports or hold at outer anchorage based on total demurrage versus fuel and operational costs.
•
Railway Rake Shortages & Evacuation Costs: When FOIS rakes are unavailable at port sidings, plants face expensive road trucking; at 80 NM out, NaviFreight pre-calculates rail vs. truck costs, evaluating bunker fuel to suggest an alternate port if fuel is ample, or the nearest port/trucking if fuel is low.
•
Vessel Type Recommendation: NaviFreight recommends the best-fit vessel for SAIL based on cargo volume and port constraints, using a What-If Simulator to assess alternative ports and identify flexible vessel types based on LOA, beam, and draft compatibility.
•
CAG Inventory Management & Carrying Cost: NaviFreight addresses CAG-identified raw-material management issues by forecasting SAIL’s future material requirements and synchronizing vessel arrivals with plant stock levels, helping prevent raw-material shortages, excess inventory, and unproductive vessel time.
•
Global Maritime Intelligence & Early Warning Radar: Tracks Baltic Dry and Capesize indices, S&P Global Platts and Argus coal price assessments, and real-time alerts across critical chokepoints (Red Sea, Malacca Strait) and Australian loading terminals (Gladstone, Hay Point) to predict freight shocks weeks in advance.
•
Coastal Triangulation & Domestic Backhaul Pairing: Analyzes draft clearance to pair discharging import bulkers with domestic coastal cargo movements (such as moving thermal coal from Paradip/Dhamra to southern power plants), eliminating unproductive ballast deadheading.
We solve the problem with an integrated, multi-modal supply chain platform tailored for Indian PSUs, combining predictive logistics, inventory security, and automated coordination. Operational modules and prototype validation are active.

Uncoordinated bulkers arrive at ports concurrently, racking up severe outer anchorage demurrage.
