2
Vessel Bunching Engine & Emergency Coal Priority Dispatching
Explicitly designed to reduce vessel idling, outer anchorage demurrage, and plant shutdown risks
TIER-1 ALERT · 48H TO 72H (2–3 DAYS OUT)
⏱️ Tier-1 Arrival ETA (2–3 Days Out)
  • Inbound vessels tracked across Bay of Bengal / Malacca / Sri Lanka gateways.
  • Queue simulator predicts simultaneous vessel arrivals at the destination berth.
  • Idling Warning: Triggers when projected waiting time exceeds 24–48 hours ($25,000+ demurrage risk).
🚨 Emergency Coal Priority Factor
  • Critical Stockpile Condition: If power/steel plant has critical inventory (<4.2 days / CEA Red Flag).
  • Emergency Priority Docking: That vessel is automatically elevated to Tier-1 priority for immediate berthing.
  • Non-Critical Vessels: Safely slow-steamed or slotted into secondary windows to save fuel and cut anchor idling.
🛡️ Vessel Idling Reduction Impact
  • Virtual Arrival Protocol: Vessels adjust speed (eco-steaming 11 kn) to arrive precisely at berth vacancy.
  • Bunker Fuel Saved: 3.2 MT VLSFO saved per idle day avoided.
  • CAG Audit Protection: Transparent timestamped demurrage justification log.
⚠️
Core Operational Rule: When plant inventory hits critical emergency, the algorithm dynamically overrides FIFO berthing, preventing costly power blackouts or blast furnace chilling while slashing overall fleet anchorage idling.
3
Critical Decision Gate & Multimodal Port Diversion
The 6-Hour Point of No Return: Anchorage Hold Demurrage vs Marine Diversion vs Rail/Truck Evacuation
CRITICAL THRESHOLD · 6 HOURS ETA
⏳ 6-Hour Point of No Return
  • Vessel approaches outer pilotage board area (80–100 NM off port).
  • Final window where course alterations incur minimal extra bunker consumption.
  • Real-time port berth availability and waiting queue locks are verified.
⚖️ 3-Way Cost & Feasibility Solver
  • COST A Hold Demurrage: ~$1,200/hr ($28,800/day) at outer roads + stockout risk.
  • COST B Port Diversion: Bunker fuel delta to adjacent port (Vizag ➔ Gangavaram, Paradip ➔ Dhamra).
  • COST C Multimodal Freight: Indian Railways FOIS rake tariff vs Road truck evacuation cost to plant.
🚦 Automated Actionable Directive
  • If Diversion Cost < Hold Cost: Issue immediate Diversion Order to Captain & Port Agent.
  • FOIS Automated Indent: Pre-books railway rakes or freight trucks at alternate port.
  • Net Result: Eliminates 3–5 days of dead vessel idling; saves $45,000–$90,000 per voyage.
🔀
Decision Formula: Net Cost = (Vessel Diversion Fuel + Alternate Port Tariff + Last-Mile Rail/Truck Rake Cost) vs (Anchorage Demurrage + Plant Starvation Penalty). The system executes diversion only when net savings are guaranteed.
4
Berth Discharge, Cargo Matcher & Coastal Hop Triangulation
Unloading bulk cargo, matching quality to consumer plants, and eliminating 100% loss return ballast voyages
STEP 4 · DISCHARGE & BACKHAUL
🏗️ Berth Discharge & Clock Stop
  • Vessel alongside wharf; grab unloader discharge operations commence (2,000 MT/hr).
  • Demurrage clock officially stopped; laytime balance calculated.
  • Direct evacuation into railway wagons or wharf conveyor stockpiles.
🎯 Cargo-to-Port Matcher
  • Evaluates coal chemistry: Ash content (%), Volatile Matter, Moisture, GCV (kcal/kg).
  • Matches parcel directly to plant boiler/blast furnace requirements (RINL Vizag, SAIL Rourkela, TATA Kalinganagar).
  • Eliminates cross-hauling and wrong-grade penalties.
🔄 Coastal Hop Triangulation (Deadhead Eliminator)
  • Problem: Empty return ballast back to Australia/Indonesia is 100% unremunerated loss.
  • Triangulation Engine (Wetzel & Tierney 2020): Finds domestic backhaul cargo along the Indian coast:
    • Coastal Thermal Coal (Paradip ➔ Ennore / Tuticorin)
    • Iron Ore Pellets / Fines (Vizag/Paradip ➔ Western India or SE Asia export).
  • Commercial Result: Converts deadhead return voyages into profitable freight legs, lowering net freight $/MT.
🌱
Backhaul Optimization: Eliminating empty ballast steaming slashes voyage carbon intensity (EEXI/CII compliant) and reduces total lifecycle bulk procurement expenditure by up to 18.4%.
